Frequently asked questions

Can you help with a single drawing?

Yes. One-off drawings are welcome, and so is regular overflow work.

Can I send a hand sketch or a PDF?

Yes. If anything is missing, such as a key dimension, I'll ask before I start.

What files will I receive?

PDF as standard, plus DWG, DXF or a 3D model if you need them.

Do you provide structural calculations or building-regulations approval?

No. I produce drawings and models from your design information.

Which areas do you cover?

Mainly Colchester, Essex and Suffolk. Drawing work from elsewhere in the UK is welcome by email.
