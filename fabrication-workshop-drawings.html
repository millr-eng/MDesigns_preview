Service overview

What this service covers.

  • Brackets, plates and joinery details
  • Dimensions and notes
  • Revision-controlled outputs

Scope, deliverables and timescales are agreed before work starts.

Drafting scope: Drawings are prepared using the design information and dimensions agreed with the client. Structural calculations, planning or building-regulations services, design certification and engineering verification are not part of the standard drafting offering. Photographs and illustrations are representative, not client projects.
