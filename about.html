About M Designs

Independent CAD drafting and 3D modelling, based in Colchester, Essex.

Background

Construction and mechanical experience

M Designs & Drafting is an independent drafting service with experience in construction and mechanical engineering, including site layouts, plant and pipework, steelwork and fabricated components.

I support small businesses and teams that need drawings produced without employing a full-time CAD technician, from single drawings to ongoing work.

Experience

  • MEng in Mechanical Engineering
  • Construction project delivery and site experience
  • Mechanical plant, pipework and fabrication
  • 2D AutoCAD drafting and SketchUp 3D modelling
