About M Designs

An independent designer you deal with directly.

M Designs & Drafting brings hands-on experience from both construction and mechanical engineering: site layouts, plant and pipework, fabricated steelwork and components.

I know how drawings get used on site and in the workshop, so I draw what the fabricator, installer or reviewer actually needs. You deal with me from first message to final file.

Many smaller firms can't justify a full-time CAD technician. I take that work off your desk, whether it's a single drawing or regular overflow when your own team is stretched.
